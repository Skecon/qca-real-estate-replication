# Data

## `raw/Real estate valuation data set.xlsx`

414 residential transactions from **Sindian District, New Taipei City, Taiwan**,
June 2012 – May 2013. Released by the UCI Machine Learning Repository under
CC BY 4.0 (<https://doi.org/10.24432/C5J30W>), donated by I-Cheng Yeh.

| Column | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `No` | row id | — |
| `X1 transaction date` | date as a decimal year (2013.250 = March 2013) | year |
| `X2 house age` | age at the transaction | year |
| `X3 distance to the nearest MRT station` | straight-line distance | metre |
| `X4 number of convenience stores` | stores in the walking circle (< 500 m) | count |
| `X5 latitude` | geographic coordinate | degree |
| `X6 longitude` | geographic coordinate | degree |
| `Y house price of unit area` | **target** | 10,000 NT$ per ping (1 ping = 3.3 m²) |

No missing values.

## This is one of the paper's four circles

Yeh & Hsu (2018) study four circles of housing supply and demand — Sindian,
Danshuei, Wunshan and Beitou. Only Sindian was released. Its descriptive
statistics reproduce the Sindian row of the paper's Table 1 exactly, which is
verified in `notebooks/01_data_and_factor_importance.ipynb` and pinned by
`tests/test_qca.py::test_dataset_matches_paper_table1`.

Two consequences for the replication:

* Weighting **methods 3 and 4** average the Eq. (8) variances over all four
  circles. They cannot be recomputed from Sindian alone, so the project uses the
  values printed in the paper's Table 3 and says so at every use.
* Any claim the paper makes about **generality across circles** cannot be
  retested here.

## Two preparation steps

Applied in `qca.data.load`:

1. **House age is floored at 0.10.** Table 1 of the paper reports a minimum age
   of 0.10 year for Sindian; the UCI release stores 17 of those transactions as
   exactly 0.0. The adjustment coefficient of house age is a logarithmic model,
   undefined at zero, so ages are floored at the value the paper itself reports
   rather than at an arbitrary epsilon.
2. **Coordinates are projected to metres.** The paper works with northing and
   easting in metres (Eq. 25); the release stores degrees. An equirectangular
   projection about the sample centroid is accurate to well under a metre over
   one district and keeps the quadratic surface well conditioned.
